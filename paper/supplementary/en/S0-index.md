## Status of the verdicts — confirmatory versus exploratory

Three of the evaluation checks reported in the manuscript are confirmatory: the retrieval-utility
check, the layer-specificity check, and the transfer check. Their preregistration documents and
verdicts appear in the crosswalk in [S6](S6-preregistration-crosswalk.md). Every other analysis is
exploratory and is labelled as such in the manuscript.

The gate discriminative-power evaluation went through four adjudications, of which only the last is
confirmatory. Why all four are reported, and what was manipulated at each, is in
[S2](S2-fault-injection-v09.md).

## This bundle produces no values of its own

The supplementary material introduces no verdicts and no figures beyond those in the manuscript.
Every value here matches the manuscript, and every table is a copy of a code-generated artefact.
